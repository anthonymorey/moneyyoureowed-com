# Money You're Owed Web Products

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

US consumers and prosumers using Money You're Owed to organize recovery, appeal, reimbursement, and self-help workflows. The Airbnb Damage Claim Builder specifically serves individual short-term-rental hosts assembling an evidence inventory after guest-caused damage.

## Product Purpose

The site publishes free guidance and paid browser products that remove execution work without promising financial, legal, insurance, platform, or recovery outcomes. The Airbnb Damage Claim Builder turns scattered facts, dates, line items, evidence labels, and correspondence into a reviewable local packet; it does not create evidence or write a claim narrative.

## Positioning

Private browser-local execution tools: customer documents and records stay on the device, deterministic software organizes the work, and official/free routes remain visible beside the paid convenience.

## Operating Context

Products are static HTML/CSS/JavaScript with Cloudflare Pages Functions for Stripe checkout, server-side entitlement verification, MailerLite capture where already approved, and Resend backup delivery. Public product pages live in the complete MYO site tree; buyer applications use no-index delivery routes. Website publication is separate from owner-controlled social and YouTube publication.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the full multi-product site tree and established checkout/delivery contracts.
- USD pricing; US-first language.
- No real charge during ordinary QA.
- No unnecessary customer-data collection and no uploading sensitive documents to AI services.
- Rules-driven products cite current primary sources, expose the source date, and fail closed on missing facts.
- A product may organize user-entered facts but may not fabricate evidence, eligibility, causation, deadlines, narratives, testimonials, savings, or success probabilities.
- Social/video publication, ads, paid research/QC, domains, and purchases remain separately gated.

## Brand Commitments

Money You're Owed is factual, direct, evidence-led, and consumer-protective. Maya is required in MYO thumbnail packaging. Public copy must state when the product is independent from a named platform and must not imply official endorsement.

## Evidence on Hand

Official policy, current terms, direct buyer-pain sources, product tests, browser screenshots, commerce contracts, deployment receipts, and canonical vault handoffs must be path-addressable. No testimonials, customer counts, recovered-money totals, or stranger-sale proof exist for the Airbnb Damage Claim Builder and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Sell organized execution, not certainty.
2. Keep private records local by default.
3. Show the free official route and the paid execution advantage honestly.
4. Separate build, page, checkout, delivery, media, publication, and sale evidence.
5. Verify the product and live custom domain before changing canonical state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable controls, clear focus, semantic structure, descriptive errors, reduced-motion support, 44px primary targets where applicable, and responsive operation at 320, 375, 414, 768, and 1280px are required.

Straight answers before checkout.

Will DenialFix tell me whether the insurer is wrong?

No. It organizes candidate wording, questions, and evidence gaps. Coverage and legal conclusions depend on the complete policy, facts, state law, expert evidence, and sometimes professional review.

Does it write an appeal letter?

It exports a preparation packet and correspondence record. It does not auto-generate a legal argument or claim that coverage exists. You can use the organized record when requesting clarification, filing a complaint, or speaking with a qualified professional.

Can I upload scanned PDFs?

Text-based PDFs work best. Image-only scans may contain no extractable text. Use your scanner’s OCR/export-to-text function, then import the searchable PDF or paste the text. Always verify against the visible original.

Does it replace a public adjuster or lawyer?

No. DenialFix does not represent you, negotiate, value loss, interpret law, or recommend a settlement. Public-adjuster licensing and lawyer rules vary by state.

What if the denial is correct?

Then the organizer may help you see the cited exclusion and stop spending time on a route that does not fit. Use the free official/nonprofit sources and seek qualified advice when the stakes or uncertainty justify it.

What is the refund policy?

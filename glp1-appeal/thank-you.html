Do this first

Keep your denial letter nearby. You will use its stated reason and filing deadline.

  1. Open “Start Here.” Match the wording in your denial notice to the decision map.
  2. Choose the matching template. Fill in the facts from your records. Do not guess or overstate anything.
  3. Collect the listed attachments. Use the prescriber worksheet when clinical information or prior authorization records are needed.
  4. File through the channel on your notice. Keep a copy of everything you send and record the date, confirmation number, and person you spoke with.

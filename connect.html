$Money You're Owed

Read-only connector

Connect your AI assistant.

The production service publishes a free public Model Context Protocol endpoint so compatible assistants can look up published, source-cited consumer money-recovery facts and point to relevant guides or self-help kits.

What it does

One tool, answer_money_question, returns published facts with original source links and a relevant site link.

  • Read-only: no create, change, delete, payment, or checkout tool.
  • No account: questions are matched in memory and are not stored according to the source policy.
  • Rate-limited: to control abusive traffic.

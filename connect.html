Connect your AI assistant

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Money You're Owed publishes a free, public MCP (Model Context Protocol) endpoint so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini can look up verified, source-cited consumer money-recovery facts — security deposits, denied refunds, denied insurance or warranty claims, and unclaimed property — and point people to our free guides and self-help kits.

What it does

One tool, answer_money_question. You ask a plain-language question; it returns published facts with their original government/legal source links, plus a link to the relevant free guide or kit on this site. That's it.

  • Read-only. There is no tool that creates, changes, deletes, or pays for anything — the code contains no transaction or checkout path at all.
  • No account, no sign-in, no personal data collected. Questions are matched in memory and are not stored. Any purchase always happens on our own checkout page at moneyyoureowed.com, never inside the assistant.
  • Rate-limited to keep the service available to everyone.

Connect URL

https://mcp.moneyyoureowed.com/mcp

This is a standard streamable-HTTP MCP server (protocol version 2025-06-18). No API key or OAuth is required.

Claude — custom connector

In Claude, go to Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector and paste the URL above.

Gemini — custom app for Spark

In Gemini, go to Connected Apps → Custom apps for Spark and paste the URL above.

ChatGPT

Money You're Owed is a verified OpenAI developer. Our ChatGPT app is informational only — it answers money-recovery questions and links to our free resources. It does not offer debt relief, debt settlement, credit repair, or any money-transfer service; it helps consumers recover money already legally owed to them. Once published, it will be reachable by name from within ChatGPT.

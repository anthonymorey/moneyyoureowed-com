Straight answers before checkout.

Will it write my Airbnb claim?

No. It creates an evidence inventory packet made of structured user-entered facts. It deliberately does not generate a guest message, reimbursement narrative, legal argument or proof.

Does it tell me whether AirCover applies?

No. The current Host Damage Protection terms define the process and eligible losses; Airbnb evaluates the request. The app only identifies empty organizer categories and shows the source date.

Are 14 and 30 days guaranteed to apply?

No. Those are the current general steps in sections 3.1.2 and 3.3 of the terms last updated August 1, 2026, rechecked September 30, 2026. Terms, account flows, facts and exceptions can change. Verify the live official terms before relying on a date.

Do I upload evidence files?

No. You record labels, sources, timestamps and what each file supports. The original file stays in your own storage.

Can I use it without buying?

You can build the same record manually using Airbnb’s free official process, a spreadsheet and your own folders. The paid workspace removes setup and organization work.
